# Things to say to Muse

You don't need to name Duesback once it's connected. Muse reaches for it whenever you talk about money you might be overpaying.

**Start**

- "Save me money."
- "Audit my bills."
- "What am I paying for that I don't use?"

**Specific**

- "Get my overdraft fees refunded."
- "Lower my internet bill."
- "Which subscriptions did I forget about?"
- "What's this charge from a company I don't recognize?"

**Ongoing**

- "Check my bills every Monday and tell me if anything changed."
- "Did my phone bill actually go down after the call?"

**Fees and account**

- "What do I owe Duesback?"
- "I don't agree with that fee."
- "Duesback, disconnect."

## What a first audit looks like

> **You:** Save me money.
> **Muse:** Found 6 things worth about $1,100 a year. Fastest win: Xfinity, $89.99 → $69.99 with one retention call. Do it?
> **You:** Yes.
> **Muse:** Done. New rate $69.99, confirmation #4471. That's $240 a year. Duesback's fee is $72, you keep $168. Approve it?

This is an illustration with made-up numbers, not a real customer.
